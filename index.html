Who we are

Cool App Helpdesk is an independent Android developer focused on building lightweight, privacy-respecting apps that just work. We publish tools for everyday productivity and educational apps designed for children.

  • All apps are free to download and use — no hidden subscriptions.
  • We believe in minimal data collection — most apps collect no data whatsoever.
  • Apps are distributed exclusively through Google Play.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetise any user information.

Our privacy commitment

Every app we publish follows the same principles:

  • No accounts required. You never need to register or log in.
  • No advertising SDKs. None of our apps display ads or use ad-tracking libraries.
  • No analytics. We do not embed crash reporters, event trackers, or usage analytics.
  • Minimal permissions. Each app requests only what it needs to function — nothing extra.
  • Transparent policies. Every app has its own plain-language privacy policy linked from the Play Store listing.
